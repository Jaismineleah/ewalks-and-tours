Our story

We started with one Land Cruiser and a lot of love for Nairobi.

E Walks And Tours began in 2014 with a simple idea: show visitors the Nairobi we grew up in — not just the postcard version. Today we run safaris, city walks and cultural days across Kenya, still guided by the same rule: treat every guest like a friend visiting home.
